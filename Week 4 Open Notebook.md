Thoughts on serindipity: 
Serendipity -> Platforms -> Playfulness

For something to be serendipitous it needs to be trigger-rich with lots of variety and the platform has to enable connection. The platforms have to be good enough to promote those connections, even as simple as showing what’s trending, fostering a sense of exploration and lead to the unexpected

Serendipity as an information-seeking method

Objects are composed as infrastructure through dynamic processes of ontological, epistemological, and social relations… become visible when they break

Omeka.org and Mukurtu.org – sites that are made for discovery in a playfulness of exploration

Your budget isn’t just busy work or fantasy, but it is a statement of where the value is

Digital History is much more fun when serendipity is involved and changes what you are looking at. Platforms we use can foster or have an impact on the serendipitous encounters that we have. 
-	Think about old Lego versus new Lego

Playfulness turns into better research in Digital History

Replication and reproduction is not the same thing
-	When we replicate something, we try to us a different data set with the same approach
-	Reproducing something is like using the same approach and same data

A lot of DH is just mucking about, but there are ways to make something replicable, reproducible, and robust and it starts with keeping track of what you are doing

Sometimes things break not because of what you’ve done, but because of the infrastructure, and the way these breaks tells us interesting things

Be resilient
